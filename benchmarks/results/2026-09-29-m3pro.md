# Full-stack benchmark — Apple M3 Pro (2026-09-28)

Run `2026-09-28T16:00:35.122Z` → `2026-09-28T17:06:06.670Z` with `benchmarks/run.sh`. Raw data and every per-turn number: `2026-09-29-m3pro.json`.

- Realtime: 200 warm turns through Chromium (headless, audio muted) in sessions of 10, cafe_order scenario, normal difficulty (end-of-turn silence 900 ms), plus one unmeasured warm-up turn. **PRD §15.3 asks for ≥ 200 turns; this run has 200** (`benchmarks/run.sh --turns 200` for the full count).
- One reply in every 3 is interrupted 600 ms into its playback by another utterance (66 barge-ins). Interrupted turns still count for response start; the reply to the interrupting utterance is reported separately (`post_barge_*`).
- Recorded practice: free answer (ASR + LLM feedback + pronunciation word timings/pitch + model audio), 30 s × 5, 120 s × 2, one job at a time. Pronunciation status per job: 30 s: timing_only × 5, 120 s: timing_only × 2.
- Learner audio: FAKE-MIC-SOURCE — macOS `say` (Samantha) fixtures scheduled into a 48 kHz AudioContext and handed to the app as its microphone MediaStream, so the app's capture worklet, resampler, WebSocket envelope, gateway VAD, ASR, LLM, TTS and playback worklet are all real. Clean synthetic speech, not learner speech: ASR/turn-taking numbers are best case.
- Clock: browser `performance.now()` for all realtime numbers. "Played" = the app's playback AudioWorklet reports the first rendered sample; the context's reported base+output latency (37 ms) comes on top at the speaker and is **not** included. No loopback recording was made.
- The machine was not otherwise idle (desktop apps, other agents); see the TTS README for how much that moves RTF.

## Reading of this run (CO-2, written by hand after the run)

- Run date is 2026-09-29 local time (KST); the heading shows the UTC date of the start timestamp.
- Load during the run: the full stack (gateway, ASR, MLX TTS, llama-server, pronunciation worker, all loaded and warm) plus
  background desktop apps, a Virtualization.framework VM (~10 % CPU) and idle coding-agent sessions. No other benchmark,
  model download or test run was active (checked with `ps` before starting).
- Unmet PRD §15.1 targets: realtime response start p50 (3.26 s vs 2 s) and p95 (4.43 s vs 4 s), and per-response TTS RTF p95
  (0.87 vs 0.8). Breakdown of the median: ~1.35 s from last voiced sample to the final transcript (includes the 900 ms
  end-of-turn silence), ~1 ms to `response.started`, ~1.9 s from `response.started` to the first audio frame (LLM first
  segment + TTS first chunk, `tts_first_chunk_ms` p50 1.17 s). The TTS first chunk alone is larger than the budget left after
  end-of-turn silence and ASR.
- Playback stalls: 50 of 134 uninterrupted replies had more than 50 ms of silence inside their playback (p95 775 ms, max
  1.4 s), mostly longer replies (20+ words). Segment gaps between sentences are small (p95 6 ms); the silence is inside
  segments, i.e. TTS generation falling behind playback (`tts_rtf_per_request` p95 1.30). PRD §15.1 has no numeric target for
  this, but it is audible.
- Met: first partial caption, barge-in stop (p95 196 ms over 66 barge-ins, 0 stale playback), recorded 30 s transcript,
  recorded 30 s and 120 s full result.
- Pronunciation: all 7 recorded jobs returned `pronunciation.status = "timing_only"` (bands off, `pronunciation_score` null) with
  word timings for every transcript word and a learner pitch contour. Learner-side step (align + prosody): p50 8.7 s for 30 s,
  36.8 s for 120 s; it runs alongside the LLM feedback call and is included in the full-result times above. For free answers
  the model-audio side (`prosody.model`) is not computed by design (only scripted exercises), so it is not measured here.
- Memory: with everything loaded the five processes use about 22.7 GB phys_footprint at the end of the run (of 36 GB);
  lifetime peaks add up to more (TTS 10.0 GB, llama-server 11.9 GB, pronunciation 6.6 GB, ASR 4.0 GB). llama-server grew from
  5.2 GB to ~10–11 GB during the first realtime sessions and then stayed flat over the remaining ~55 minutes (20 sessions of 10
  turns); this is not the PRD's 10-session memory-reclaim test, which is still not measured.
- Not measured: stop button, 60-minute stability with cancel/retry/mode switch, 10-session memory reclaim, loopback-recorded
  playback timing, WER on learner speech. Learner audio is synthetic (`say`), so nothing here says anything about Korean
  learners or pronunciation accuracy.
